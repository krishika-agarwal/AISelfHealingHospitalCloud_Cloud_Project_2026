# Literature Survey

A comprehensive literature survey was conducted by reviewing 15 research papers related to cloud computing, AIOps, Explainable AI (XAI), anomaly detection, self-healing cloud infrastructure, and Hospital Information Systems.

The survey focuses on:

- AI-driven anomaly detection in cloud environments
- Explainable AI techniques for root cause analysis
- Self-healing cloud infrastructure
- Cloud monitoring and automated recovery mechanisms
- AWS-based healthcare cloud solutions
- Existing limitations and research gaps

A detailed comparative analysis of all 15 research papers, including methodology, advantages, limitations, and identified research gaps, is provided in the submitted Phase-I project report.
